# Unlock My Home — Product Brief

## Positioning
A premium, trusted property discovery marketplace. Launch geography: Hyderabad and nearby growth corridors. Product scope: properties for sale only.

## Audiences
- Buyers: discover, filter, shortlist, enquire and request site visits.
- Dealers: manage listings and respond to leads.
- Direct sellers: apply to list a property, subject to moderation.
- Super Admin: control users, listings, verification, enquiries and promotions.

## MVP features
1. Public website with SEO-friendly property detail pages.
2. Search by locality, property type, budget and land area.
3. Listing cards with price, area, location and status.
4. Dealer invitation and approval workflow.
5. Property submission with mandatory fields and media.
6. Admin moderation before publication.
7. Buyer enquiries and site-visit requests.
8. Dealer lead inbox.
9. Admin dashboard, listing management and audit trail.
10. Responsive design and analytics.

## Trust principles
- No rental listings in MVP.
- No fake/sample properties in production.
- No blanket legal-title guarantee.
- Verification badge must describe the exact checks completed.
- Private documents must never be exposed through public listing URLs.
- Dealer access must be isolated by database policies.

## Suggested launch sequence
Pilot with 5–10 known dealers and 20–50 authorised listings. Run test enquiries, verify response times and listing accuracy, then scale marketing.
